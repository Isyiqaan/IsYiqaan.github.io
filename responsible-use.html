Responsible Use

Enjoy the result—keep it in perspective

A personality quiz can be a fun prompt for reflection, but a short online experience cannot define a whole person. Use these principles when reading or sharing your result.

Treat it as entertainment

The result is not a diagnosis, professional assessment, intelligence test, or prediction. A high or low percentage is not proof of ability, character, mental health, or future behavior.

Look for questions, not verdicts

Instead of asking “Is this exactly who I am?”, try asking “When do I act this way?” or “Would a friend see this differently?” Reflection is more useful when it leaves room for context and change.

Do not use it to make high-stakes decisions

Do not use results to choose medical care, employment, education, legal action, finances, or whether to end a relationship. For serious concerns, speak with an appropriately qualified professional.

Share respectfully

Only share another person's name or result with their permission. Avoid using playful labels to embarrass, bully, exclude, or pressure someone. WhatsApp sharing is optional and opens only when you choose the share button.

Protect your privacy

The site stores progress in your browser. Use a nickname if you do not want your real name displayed on the device or included in a shared message. Clear the browser's site data when using a shared or public computer.

Remember that people change

Personality is expressed differently across family, work, friendship, stress, culture, and time. No single session captures all of those contexts. Different results are expected and should not be treated as contradictions.

For parents and younger visitors

The quiz is general-audience entertainment and does not ask for an account. Parents or guardians should help younger visitors understand advertising, external links, and the difference between a game result and professional advice.
