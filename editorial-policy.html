Trust & Transparency

Editorial Policy

We want visitors and reviewers to know who the content is for, how it is produced, and how mistakes are corrected.

Purpose

Our content supports a free entertainment quiz and helps visitors interpret it responsibly. We aim for clear, original explanations written for a general audience. We do not present entertainment scores as science or professional advice.

Writing standards

Guides should use complete sentences, define unfamiliar ideas, distinguish fact from opinion, and state limitations near the relevant claim. We avoid exaggerated promises such as guaranteed accuracy, mind reading, diagnosis, or prediction.

Sources and accuracy

When a page discusses an external rule or service, we prefer the official source. General explanations of this quiz are based on the site's actual code and behavior. We review pages after functional changes so instructions do not describe features that no longer exist.

Advertising independence

Advertising does not determine quiz outcomes, trait percentages, rankings, or editorial wording. Ads should be visually distinguishable from navigation and should never be described as a required step.

Corrections

Visitors can report an error through the Contact page. Useful reports identify the page, the incorrect wording, and the expected correction. We may revise content for accuracy, clarity, accessibility, or policy compliance without changing the entertainment purpose of the quiz.

Ownership and contact

Ultimate Personality Test is operated by the owner of this website. Questions about content, privacy, or corrections can be sent to khalidcabdala74@gmail.com.
